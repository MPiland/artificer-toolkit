### BUG

Opportunity attacks never prompt. Steps: as GM in an Assisted Encounter (review data set 0), drag Wolf's token next to Cade's, then drag it more than 5 feet away. The move settles at once with no Pending decision, whoever is nearby and whatever the Reaction time setting. Prone, reaction use, reach and visibility are not the cause: the engine only offers an opportunity attack to a reactor that has a reaction action whose `reactionTrigger.kind` is `MOVEMENT`. For Characters that is `encounterMovementReactionSources.ts:128-134`; for monsters it is the feature capabilities found by `discoverMovementReactionCapabilities` (`encounterMovementReactionSources.ts:175-205`). No SRD Character class, spell, basic action or creature authors such a reaction: a search of `packages/source-data/src` finds no `kind: 'MOVEMENT'` reaction trigger and no `eligibilityDefinitionId` (only Disengage and Flyby immunity, `basicActions.ts:300` and `creatures/shared.ts:450`). Movement reaction tests use their own fixtures (`apps/apis/tests/fixtures/movementReaction*.ts`), so they pass without production data. The engine's D&D policy (`reactionEligibilityPolicy.ts:88-114`) does not check Prone. No code was changed. The guide now says none is offered and lists it under Known gaps.
