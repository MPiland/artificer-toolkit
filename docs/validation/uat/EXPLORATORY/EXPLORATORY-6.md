# EXPLORATORY-6: Find out what went wrong

Work out why something at the table did not behave as you expected, and fix it or report it.

Level 3 (Core play) · Feature EXPLORATORY · Mode: Group (P2 is the tester, GM follows the script) · Path position 87 of 191

Do not open the scripted scenario files before your Result is written: they name every button and would hide the confusion this task exists to find. This task is not pass/fail.

## Prerequisites

- The account **P2** exists and is seated in The Campaign. P2 has Bryn as their Campaign Character: a Level 1 Fighter that P2 built in the Character Builder.
- **The Campaign** is hosted by GM, with a Wolf and a Goblin and an Encounter running that includes Bryn.

## Accounts and windows

- **Roles used:** P2, GM
- **Windows:** Each role is a different person in their own browser, signed in to their own account.
- **Tester:** P2, in their own browser. You never act as another role.
- **GM:** a second person, in their own browser and signed in to their own GM account, following the script below. They do only what the script says, when the trigger happens, and do not explain controls.

## Script for the second person

1. Before the tester starts: GM opens The Campaign.
2. When the tester says it is their turn or asks for it: GM makes Bryn the active actor.
3. When the tester says they want to try something that needs the GM (a ruling, a hidden or moved token, a condition): GM does exactly what is asked and nothing more.
4. If the tester asks why something happened, GM answers only by pointing at what the app shows (log, messages), as the tester should find out without outside help.

## Task

- **Goal:** Work out why something at the table did not behave as you expected, and fix it or report it.
- **Scope:** Attempt something you think the rules allow but are unsure the product supports (for example an unusual action, a reaction, or moving through difficult terrain). If the result surprises you, find out why using only what the app shows. End when you can explain the result, or decide it is a defect.
- **Look for:** whether the app explained its own result; where you went to find the reason; whether the log or message wording matched your mental model of the rules.

Follow [how to run a task](README.md#how-to-run-a-task) and write the Result below when you stop. Tell the second person in words when a trigger in the script happens (for example "my Character is ready").

## Afterwards

- GM: undo any damage or movement caused, through the normal controls.
