# UAT-ORIENTATION-1: Sign in and find your Campaigns and Characters

Sign in with the account you were given and check that you see what the role table in the Start here page says that role should see.

Level 0 (Orientation and exploratory tasks) · Feature ORIENTATION · Mode: Solo (any one role) · Path position 1 of 188

## Prerequisites

- The review data provides the account for **your role** (GM, P1, P2, P3 or SPARE) in your set, with the sign-in details the owner gave you privately, and the four review Campaigns copied for your set.
- The review data provides the staging address `<STAGING_URL>`, set in the [Start here page](../README.md#start).

## Accounts and windows

- **Roles used:** the one role you were assigned (repeat the scenario for each role if the owner asks)
- **Windows:** One browser window, your normal browser.

## Steps

1. Open `<STAGING_URL>`, choose sign in, and sign in with your account's email or username and password.
2. Open **Campaigns** and write down every Campaign you see.
3. Open **Characters** and write down every Character you see.
4. If you are the GM, open each of the four Campaigns in turn and find the Party panel, the Live tabletop panel and the Sessions list.
5. Sign out, then sign in again with the same details.

## Expected

- Step 1: you reach the signed-in home page; no error.
- Steps 2 and 3: you see exactly what the [role table](../README.md#roles-and-what-each-sees) lists for your role, nothing more.
- Step 4: each Campaign shows the three panels.
- Step 5: sign-out and sign-in both work and the lists are the same.

## Other users see

- Not applicable: single user.
