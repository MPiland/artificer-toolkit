## Scope

T1, T2 and T3 together close AC SPELLS-28.1:

- T1, TELEPORT: Word of Recall, and the travel half of Plane Shift.
- T2, MANIFEST: Giant Insect.
- T3, TELEPATHIC_LINK: Telepathic Bond.

Each outcome executor is authored from core options. Each needs a test against the real compiler, and the AC needs one journey. The compiler-derived corpus rule from SPELLS-26 (AC 26.1) applies: these spells must stop being excluded for an unsupported outcome.
