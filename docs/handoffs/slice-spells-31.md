### Vocabulary checked for the class B rulings

- `ABILITY_SCORE` takes BONUS, PENALTY and SET_VALUE only (`modifier.ts:256`); no cap exists. `ABILITY_CHECK` and `ATTACKS_AGAINST` take ADV and DIS only, with no opportunity-attack qualifier and no advantage suppression. `RANGE` takes REPLACE_RANGE_KIND only; `DURATION` has no multiply. The PROFICIENCY_BONUS value source has a `multiplier` but no rounding control (`modifier.ts:437`).
- Halving damage on a save is source-side only (SAVE_SUCCESS_OUTCOME_KIND NONE/HALF); nothing halves or reduces incoming damage on the holder's side, and nothing rerolls a die or a d20.
- No restriction predicate means "while another effect is active" (Rage benefits live inside the Rage APPLY_GRANT, `barbarian.ts:281`). Lifecycle end events have no on-move, on-action or on-reaction event. No disease or turned condition exists.
- Senses support `illuminationAccess` including MAGICAL_DARKNESS (`senses.ts:116`), so Devil's Sight is class A.
- Eldritch Blast, Scorching Ray, Arcane Sword and Spiritual Weapon already author per-creature attacks, which is why Volley and Whirlwind Attack are proposed as class A.

### Authoring gaps found (fixed inside the owning subtask)

- No `resourceUse` on Bardic Inspiration, Cutting Words, Peerless Skill, Turn the Unholy, Preserve Life or Draconic Presence.
- Sacred Weapon spends Holy Nimbus's charge (`oath-of-devotion.ts`): a likely bug, regression test in T4e.
- No charges authored for Divine Sense, Lay on Hands, Cleansing Touch, Stroke of Luck or Eldritch Master.
- Aura of Courage and Aura of Devotion lack the 18th-level 30 ft range.
- College of Lore's Additional Magical Secrets rule sits on the Bonus Proficiencies feature (deleted in T2; the 6th-level feature already grants the spells).
- Fighter Extra Attack lacks 3 attacks at 11th and 4 at 20th (`fighter.ts:759`); outside the closed-rule scope, so a SPELLS-31 follow-up unless the orchestrator folds it into T3a.
- The cleric description omits the Turn Undead text (T4m).
