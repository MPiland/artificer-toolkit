# UAT-RULES-4: Healing keeps its dice and modifier visible in the roll card

Healing keeps its dice and modifier visible in the roll card.

Level 3 (Core play) · Feature RULES · Mode: Group (GM, P1) · Path position 115 of 191

## Prerequisites

- **The Campaign** is hosted by GM with P1, P2 and P3 seated as players.
- **The Campaign** has Aria (P1), Bryn (P2) and Cade (P3), level 1 Fighters each player built, seated; Assisted mode with the Speed Factor initiative; and a running Encounter in round 2 with tokens for the three Fighters, a Wolf and a Goblin, as the GM built it in [UAT-ENCOUNTER-1](../ENCOUNTER/UAT-ENCOUNTER-1.md) (steps 12 to 16) and played on.

## Accounts and windows

- **Roles used:** GM, P1
- **Windows:** Each role is a different person in their own browser, signed in to their own account.

## Steps

Mark each step: `[P]` passed, `[F]` failed, `[-]` not reached. Leave `[ ]` until run.

S1. **GM:** If Aria is at full hit points, damage her through the Encounter first.

1. [ ] **P1:** Open the tabletop of The Campaign and wait for Aria's turn (advance turns as GM if needed).
2. [ ] **P1:** Note Aria's current hit points.
3. [ ] **P1:** Open the action drawer and choose Second Wind (or the heal available).
4. [ ] **P1:** At the roll prompt, choose **Roll dice**.
5. [ ] **P1:** Read the roll card shown in chat.
6. [ ] **P1:** Note Aria's hit points afterwards.

## Expected

- The roll card shows the dice (for example 1d10) and the added modifier separately, then the total.
- Hit points rise by that total, capped at maximum.
- One heal produces one roll card, not two.

## Other users see

- **GM:** Sees the same roll card in chat and Aria's updated hit points.
