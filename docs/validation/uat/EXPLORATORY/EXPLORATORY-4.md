# EXPLORATORY-4: Run a short session as a first-time player

Join a Campaign you have never seen, find your Character and take your first turn.

Level 0 (Orientation and exploratory tasks) · Feature EXPLORATORY · Mode: Solo (SPARE) with a facilitator playing GM · Path position 5 of 188

Do not open the scripted scenario files before your Result is written: they name every button and would hide the confusion this task exists to find. This task is not pass/fail.

## Prerequisites

- The review data provides the account SPARE, who is a member of no Campaign and owns no Character.
- The review data provides the Campaign **Review: Shield and Hold** (Shield and Hold Person, Encounter running), owned by GM.

## Roles and windows

- **Roles used:** SPARE, GM
- **Tester role:** SPARE. You use one account from your set, in your normal browser, and you never act as another role.
- **Facilitator:** the owner, signed in as GM in a separate browser profile, following the script below. The facilitator does only what the script says, when the trigger happens, and does not explain controls.

## Facilitator script

1. Before the tester starts: GM signs in in a separate profile, opens Review: Shield and Hold and invites SPARE by username.
2. When the tester says they accepted the invitation: GM assigns SPARE a seat and a Character (build one if the tester asks), and tells the tester.
3. When the tester says they are ready to play: GM makes SPARE's Character the active actor and tells the tester when it is their turn.

## Task

- **Goal:** Join a Campaign you have never seen, find your Character and take your first turn.
- **Scope:** Accept the invitation, open the Campaign, and take a turn in the running Encounter. You may use the [alpha play guide](../../../guides/alpha-play-guide.md). If you need a Character, build one.
- **Look for:** whether the invitation and joining steps were clear; whether you could tell whose turn it was; whether you could find the action you wanted without help.

Follow [how to run a task](README.md#how-to-run-a-task) and write the Result below when you stop. Tell the facilitator in words when a trigger in the script happens (for example "my Character is ready").

## Afterwards

- GM: remove SPARE from Review: Shield and Hold (Party panel) and delete any Character built for SPARE, so SPARE is again in no Campaign.

## ACs covered

None. Exploratory tasks check usability and bias, not acceptance criteria.
