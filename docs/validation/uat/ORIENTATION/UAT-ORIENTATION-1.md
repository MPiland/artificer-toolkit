# UAT-ORIENTATION-1: Sign in and look around your account

Sign in with your own account and check that it works and that nothing belongs to it yet.

Level 0 (Orientation and exploratory tasks) · Feature ORIENTATION · Mode: Solo (any one role) · Path position 1 of 191

## Prerequisites

- You have the sign-in details of your own account (GM, P1, P2, P3, P4 or P5), given to you privately. At this point no Campaign or Character exists yet: they are created in later scenarios.
- You have the staging address `<STAGING_URL>`, set in the [Start here page](../README.md#start).

## Accounts and windows

- **Roles used:** your own role (GM, P1, P2, P3, P4 or P5)
- **Windows:** One browser, signed in as your own role.

## Steps

Mark each step: `[P]` passed, `[F]` failed, `[-]` not reached. Leave `[ ]` until run.

1. [ ] Open `<STAGING_URL>`, choose sign in, and sign in with your account's email or username and password.
2. [ ] Open **Campaigns** and write down everything you see.
3. [ ] Open **Characters** and write down everything you see.
4. [ ] Open Settings and Account and check that the username and email shown are yours.
5. [ ] Sign out, then sign in again with the same details.

## Expected

- Step 1: you reach the signed-in home page; no error.
- Steps 2 and 3: both lists are empty and show a plain empty-state message, not an error.
- Step 4: the username and email are the ones you were given.
- Step 5: sign-out and sign-in both work and the lists are the same.

## ACs covered

None. This scenario checks that your account works; it covers no acceptance criterion.
