## ATTACK-6.T3 (AC ATTACK-6.2)

- Seam trace: the SRD source audit is `auditSrdSourceSemantics` (`packages/source-data/src/srd/audit.ts`), run by `audit.test.ts` and by the SRD manifest generator (`apps/apis/scripts/generateSrdManifest.ts`), which fails on any issue. The Creature schema (`creatureEquipmentLoadoutDtoSchema`, `validateCreatureEquipmentLoadout`) checks entry identity, quantity, action bindings and hand conflicts without Equipment, so it cannot know two-handed weapons or categories.
- The audit now runs `auditCreatureEquipmentLoadouts` over every SRD Creature, under the `creature-equipment` domain, with five named checks (D107 and its AUTHORING-17 addendum): `loadout binds missing Equipment`; `loadout hand conflict` (the shared `resolveHandOccupancy` with `TWO_HANDED` read from the bound Equipment); `loadout holds an item that is not a weapon or shield, or wears one that is` (the shared `isHeldEquipmentCategory`); `loadout armor binds an action Feature`; `loadout wears more than one armor`. Carried items are not category-checked, and a shield may bind an action (the spiked shield attack).
- Proof: `packages/source-data/src/srd/creatureEquipmentLoadoutAudit.test.ts`: every SRD loadout passes; the SRD Goblin with one fault per check yields exactly that named issue on the faulty entries.

## ATTACK-6.T4a (AC ATTACK-6.3, first of two tasks)

- Seam audit and split: `attack-6-t4-split-proposal.md` in the orchestrator's session notes; T4b (weapon attack and damage, room journey) closes ATTACK-6.3.
- One Equipment fold: `buildGrantFoldEquipmentFacts` (`character/characterGrantFoldSourceFacts.ts`) maps loadout or inventory items to grant-fold base equipment and fold items, for Characters (`characterGrantFoldRuleFacts.ts`) and Creatures (`foldDnd5eCreatureEquipment`, `creatureFeatureGrantFold.ts`, holder `CREATURE`). A worn or held Creature entry is equipped, a carried one is not, and a Creature is attuned to every loadout item.
- One Armor Class path: the Character Armor Class block of `evaluateDnd5eCharacterVitals` is `resolveDnd5eArmorClass`; `resolveDnd5eCreatureArmorClass` (now in `characterVitals.ts`, same adapter signature except `abilityScores` replaces `dexterityScore`) folds the loadout, evaluates its modifier groups with `evaluateDnd5eCharacterModifiers` and adds natural armor. The Creature-only while-equipped path in `armorClass.ts` is deleted; D107's AUTHORING-17 addendum is amended.
- Intentional behavior change: an Equipment modifier without the while-equipped restriction no longer refuses a Creature's Armor Class; it applies as it does for a Character (`creatureArmorClass.test.ts`, "applies a held magic shield modifier that carries no restriction").
- Ruling (orchestrator, 2026-10-26, D152 amended): SRD item modifiers are authored in CHARACTER contexts, which D152 gated to Character holders. A context of only source-state predicates (`WHILE_SOURCE_*`) now skips the holder-kind gate through `describesOnlySource` (`characterGrantFoldRestrictionFacts.ts`), shared with the CREATURE source-only rule; holder predicates stay Character-only.
- Proofs (`packages/rules-runtime/src/__tests__/creatureArmorClass.test.ts`, "a Character and a Creature with the same magic item (ATTACK-6.3)"): Breastplate +2 and Shield +1 give both 18 and 21; a held Shield +1 gives both 16 and a carried one 13 (its while-equipped restriction refuses for both); a Shield +1 whose bonus requires worn armor (`EQUIPMENT_EQUIPPED`, a holder predicate) applies to the Character with Leather Armor (17) and is refused on the Creature (16), as D152 says.
- Re-mapped "cannot evaluate" tests (the refusal no longer exists): `creatureEquipmentVariantPin.test.ts` now pins the remaining refusal, a held shield without an armor class (D143); `creaturePublication.test.ts` reads and publishes AC 15 for an archmage with an unrestricted +1 shield; `equipmentCommands.test.ts` admits that Creature and recomputes its AC on replacement like the SRD +1 shield.

## ATTACK-6.T4b (AC ATTACK-6.3, closes it with T4a)

- Seam trace: a Creature Feature's attack is discovered in `encounterFeatureActionCapabilityDiscovery.ts` (`featureAttackMechanic`, re-derived by the staleness check before a resolve), bound to its loadout weapon by `entryEquipmentBinding`, and its damage dice are bound under the Encounter lock by `resolveCreatureDamageExpression` (`effectActionCompiler.ts`) from the outcomes `featureActionMechanic` emits. Neither the attack mechanic nor the action mechanic outcomes are stored behind a Convex or schema validator: no stored-shape change.
- One modifier path: `dnd5eActionModifierGroups` (`characterModifiers.ts`) builds the action-model modifier groups for Characters (`characterPolicy.ts`) and Creatures, and `dnd5eAttackBonusTotal` (`characterActionMechanics.ts`) sums the applied `ATTACK_ROLL`/`DAMAGE` BONUS and PENALTY contributions that apply to an attacking item, for a Character's attack and a Creature's alike.
- Creature side: `evaluateDnd5eCreatureEquipment` (`creatureFeatureGrantFold.ts`) is the folded loadout plus its modifier groups, shared with the Creature Armor Class; `resolveDnd5eCreatureWeaponBonuses` evaluates them against the bound loadout entry as the attacking item (its `EQUIPMENT_INSTANCE` source, Equipment identities, category, properties, the Feature's grip). It is the attack-profile adapter's `creatureWeaponBonuses`.
- Discovery adds the bound weapon's attack bonus to the authored ATTACK roll's fixed modifier and carries its `damageBonus` on the attack mechanic; staleness compares both. `featureActionMechanic` puts `damageBonus` on the first DAMAGE outcome, as a Character's attack damage bonus joins its first damage, and `resolveCreatureDamageExpression` adds it. Ability and proficiency stay the stat block's: SRD loadouts bind only mundane weapons, whose only grants are ability modifiers outside these domains, so the print is unchanged (the SRD Creature attack sweep holds).
- Shared Creature ability scores read: `snapshotAbilityScores` (`encounterActorState.ts`) serves the Creature Armor Class projection and the weapon bonuses.
- Proofs:
  - `packages/rules-runtime/src/__tests__/creatureWeaponBonuses.test.ts`: a held Longsword +1 gives a Creature the +1/+1 it gives a Character (difference to the mundane Longsword); a mundane weapon adds nothing; a carried +1 weapon is unequipped and adds nothing. The seeded Ogre's Greatclub action keeps +6 and 2d8+4, and holding a Greatclub +1 discovers +7 with damage 2d8+5.
  - Room journey, `apps/session-worker/test/nativeRoomEquipmentReplacement.test.ts` ("a replaced magic weapon's bonuses in the Ogre's attack"): the GM replaces the Ogre's Greatclub with a Greatclub +1, and the Ogre's attack settles with d20 modifier 7 and one more damage than with its Greatclub.
- AC mapping: ATTACK-6.3 is closed by T4a (Armor Class) and T4b (weapon attack and damage).
