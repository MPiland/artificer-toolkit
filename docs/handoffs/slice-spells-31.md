### Vocabulary checked for the class B rulings

- `ABILITY_SCORE` takes BONUS, PENALTY and SET_VALUE only (`modifier.ts:256`); no cap exists. `ABILITY_CHECK` and `ATTACKS_AGAINST` take ADV and DIS only, with no opportunity-attack qualifier and no advantage suppression. `RANGE` takes REPLACE_RANGE_KIND only; `DURATION` has no multiply. The PROFICIENCY_BONUS value source has a `multiplier` but no rounding control (`modifier.ts:437`).
- Halving damage on a save is source-side only (SAVE_SUCCESS_OUTCOME_KIND NONE/HALF); nothing halves or reduces incoming damage on the holder's side, and nothing rerolls a die or a d20.
- No restriction predicate means "while another effect is active" (Rage benefits live inside the Rage APPLY_GRANT, `barbarian.ts:281`). Lifecycle end events have no on-move, on-action or on-reaction event. No disease or turned condition exists.
- Senses support `illuminationAccess` including MAGICAL_DARKNESS (`senses.ts:116`), so Devil's Sight is class A.
- Eldritch Blast, Scorching Ray, Arcane Sword and Spiritual Weapon already author per-creature attacks, which is why Volley and Whirlwind Attack are proposed as class A.

### Authoring gaps found (fixed inside the owning subtask)

- No `resourceUse` on Bardic Inspiration, Cutting Words, Peerless Skill, Turn the Unholy, Preserve Life or Draconic Presence.
- Sacred Weapon spends Holy Nimbus's charge (`oath-of-devotion.ts`): a likely bug, regression test in T4e. Fixed in T4e: Oath of Devotion now has a Channel Divinity feature with one short-rest charge that Sacred Weapon and Turn the Unholy both spend; `classFeatureCoreOptions.test.ts` pins both.
- No charges authored for Divine Sense, Lay on Hands, Cleansing Touch, Stroke of Luck or Eldritch Master.
- Aura of Courage and Aura of Devotion lack the 18th-level 30 ft range.
- College of Lore's Additional Magical Secrets rule sits on the Bonus Proficiencies feature (deleted in T2; the 6th-level feature already grants the spells).
- Fighter Extra Attack lacks 3 attacks at 11th and 4 at 20th (`fighter.ts:759`); outside the closed-rule scope, so a SPELLS-31 follow-up unless the orchestrator folds it into T3a.
- The cleric description omits the Turn Undead text (T4m).

### Tranquility resume (T4k2d, ruled 2026-10-19)

The LONG_REST_FINISHED trigger ruling is withdrawn; do not add it. `CLASS_WAY_OF_THE_OPEN_HAND_TRANQUILITY` (`way-of-the-open-hand.ts`, feature `classRowId(M,11,3)`) is still a closed rule.

Findings that ruled out a rest-time invocation:

1. Active effects are created only by the encounter planner (`prepareRecurringActiveEffect`), which stamps an ENCOUNTER origin and needs an encounter, occurrence and source actor. COMPLETE_REST in `sheetCommands.ts` has none, so a Sheet-side Character-origin planner would be new.

3. `heldIncomingGate` throws for an effect ward without an ENCOUNTER origin and `sourceActorId`; only the feature-fold (holder-owned) ward avoids that.

Design B (ruled): no rest hook, no planner, no schema relaxation.

- Tranquility is a holder-owned INCOMING_ATTACK_GATE feature grant (the Nature's Sanctuary shape), DERIVED_DC Wisdom: 8 + Wisdom modifier + proficiency bonus, owner = holder.

**Product ruling (user, 2026-10-19) supersedes Design B's "unspent charge" activation:**

- The Sheet shows the use as spent and the ward as active or ended.
- This needs a real Character-side active status outside encounters. Reuse an existing Character activation path if one exists; otherwise add a generic one (no Tranquility branch). The three findings above are why the encounter-planner path cannot carry it.

### T5o2 seam notes (Thief's Reflexes, `CLASS_THIEF_THIEFS_REFLEXES`; size 3, depends on T5o)

> **Superseded by D187a (2026-10-23).** Thief's Reflexes shipped as a passive MANUAL_ADJUDICATION GM ruling and `CLASS_THIEF_THIEFS_REFLEXES` is deleted. The EXTRA_TURN engine piece is not built; it is listed in future-enhancements (Deferred rules pieces). The notes below describe that deferred piece.

Thief's Reflexes (thief.ts:130): in the first round of each combat the thief takes two turns, the second at initiative minus 10. Nothing of it is built in T5o.

- **Trigger:** reuse `TRIGGER.ROLL_INITIATIVE` (T5o) as a PASSIVE effect with `PATH_CODE.PASSIVE`; the new outcome is `EXTRA_TURN { initiativeOffset: -10, round: 1 }` (names to be ruled). Do not add a second trigger.
- **Where the hook lives:** T5o's stage runs in `persistEncounterTurnCommit` after the turn's own writes, when `root.initiative` is non-null, and reads `initiativeRestoreSources` from the Character's rule evaluation (`characterActionRuleFacts.ts`). EXTRA_TURN needs the same discovery (a sibling `initiativeExtraTurns` sink next to `initiativeRestores`), but must act at the settlement itself: the order is planned and staged (`persistStagedInitiativeRound` in `turnCommitPersistence.ts`) before T5o's stage runs, and the extra entry is part of `orderedActorIds` and the normalized initiative round (`initiativeStore.ts`). It therefore belongs in that planning, not in a post-settlement stage like the resource restore.
- **Initiative model:** one entry per actor today (`orderedActorIds`, `turnOrderOrdinal`). A second entry for the same actor needs a participant key that is not the actor id (see `actorReinforcementInitiative.ts` for the insert-into-current-round pattern) and a cursor that tolerates a repeated actor (`encounterInitiativeCursor.ts` `validateInitiativeCursor`).
- **Per-turn state:** an actor's turn resources reset on each turn start, and the second entry starts a second turn: check `turnResourcesPersistence.ts` and the end-of-turn effect ticks (a duration counted in the actor's turns would tick twice).
- **Cadence:** round 1 only. An EACH_ROUND re-roll must not add the entry again, and SIDE_ROLL procedures need a ruling (the extra entry has no side roll of its own).
- **Proof shape:** authoring parity plus a room journey with the thief acting twice in round 1 and once in round 2.
