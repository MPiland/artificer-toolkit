# SPELLS-8.T7: multi-Effect spells cast in an Encounter (D187a)

- Status: implemented on `spells-8-t7`; CI is the gate.
- Finding: after T2v2a3d (Spiritual Weapon) and SPELLS-4.T2w (Faithful Hound) none of the four spells needs a multi-group cast offer. Faithful Hound is one Effect. Grease is one Effect (its second group, the appear save, is a path of it) and casts in the room (`nativeRoomHazardZoneSpells.test.ts`). Spiritual Weapon's sibling attack Effect is repeated by the cast's offer and never yields a cast action of its own. `castableEffectGroups` is unchanged.
- Arcane Sword: authored like Spiritual Weapon. The cast summons an invulnerable, space-free `SUMMON` sword (Concentration, 1 minute) and a sibling melee spell attack Effect (3d10 force) is repeated through a `CONJURED` `REPEAT_SOURCE_PATH` offer: free once on the cast turn, then a bonus action. Moving the sword up to 20 feet with that bonus action is a GM ruling (D190a). The `WEAPON` manifest is no longer authored by any spell; the kind stays in the generic manifest vocabulary beside `SENSOR`, `OBJECT` and `ILLUSION` and is not a closed rule.
- Bug fixed here (found by the Arcane Sword room proof, which was rejected before the fix): an area cast that summons under Concentration was refused by the area receipt ("An area root carries a maintained effect only for the zone it placed"). The refine in `ruleExecutionEncounterApplications.ts` now also accepts the carrier effect the cast leaves on its source (`sourceGrantTransition`), which is what holds a summon.
- Proof: `coreOptionSpellAuthoringParity.test.ts` (Arcane Sword; Faithful Hound from T2w); room `nativeRoomCoreOptionSpells.test.ts` "Arcane Sword": the cast conjures an owned sword, the free attack deals 3d10 force, revert; the compile golden (Arcane Sword's attack Effect).
