# EXPLORATORY-6: Find out what went wrong

Work out why something at the table did not behave as you expected, and fix it or report it.

Level 0 (Orientation and exploratory tasks) · Feature EXPLORATORY · Mode: Solo (P2) with a facilitator playing GM · Path position 7 of 188

Do not open the scripted scenario files before your Result is written: they name every button and would hide the confusion this task exists to find. This task is not pass/fail.

## Prerequisites

- The review data provides the account P2, who owns Pyra and is a member of Review: Fire and Thunder.
- The review data provides the Campaign **Review: Fire and Thunder** (Pyra, Wolf and three Goblins; Encounter running), owned by GM.

## Roles and windows

- **Roles used:** P2, GM
- **Tester role:** P2. You use one account from your set, in your normal browser, and you never act as another role.
- **Facilitator:** the owner, signed in as GM in a separate browser profile, following the script below. The facilitator does only what the script says, when the trigger happens, and does not explain controls.

## Facilitator script

1. Before the tester starts: GM signs in in a separate profile and opens Review: Fire and Thunder.
2. When the tester says it is their turn or asks for it: GM makes Pyra the active actor.
3. When the tester says they want to try something that needs the GM (a ruling, a hidden or moved token, a condition): GM does exactly what is asked and nothing more.
4. If the tester asks why something happened, GM answers only by pointing at what the app shows (log, messages), as the tester should find out without outside help.

## Task

- **Goal:** Work out why something at the table did not behave as you expected, and fix it or report it.
- **Scope:** Attempt something you think the rules allow but are unsure the product supports (for example an unusual spell target, a reaction, or moving through difficult terrain). If the result surprises you, find out why using only what the app shows. End when you can explain the result, or decide it is a defect.
- **Look for:** whether the app explained its own result; where you went to find the reason; whether the log or message wording matched your mental model of the rules.

Follow [how to run a task](README.md#how-to-run-a-task) and write the Result below when you stop. Tell the facilitator in words when a trigger in the script happens (for example "my Character is ready").

## Afterwards

- GM: undo any damage or movement caused, through the normal controls.

## ACs covered

None. Exploratory tasks check usability and bias, not acceptance criteria.
