## Scope

- AC SPELLS-4.2: every SRD hazard zone spell places a zone whose entry and turn rules are authored from core options and resolve in Encounters.
- No spell-specific code: each zone is authored data compiled by the generic placed-zone compiler (`placedSpaceZone`, `zonePlacementPlan`). A rule the compiler does not model stays unauthored and is named below, never partly honored.

### T2e (a grant bound to a chosen held item, D156 T2 class B)

- New core options, generic and data-authored: a path's `chosenItem` (category, required and excluded properties, named equipment), `bindsChosenItem` on `APPLY_GRANT` (its SOURCE-scoped modifiers apply only to that item's attacks), `unlessChosenItemReleased` on `APPLY_GRANT`, and a `RELEASE_CHOSEN_ITEM` outcome that lets the item go through Disarm's equipment change (held items only; worn armor is never released). `WEAPON_DAMAGE_DIE` `SET_VALUE`, `ATTACK_TRAIT` `ENABLE` (MAGICAL bypass) and `SPELLCASTING` on `OPTIONAL_ABILITY_MODIFIER_BONUS` complete the grant vocabulary. Node-map fields: `pathChosenItem` group on the path, the two `APPLY_GRANT` toggles.
- The release event is `ON_CHOSEN_ITEM_RELEASED` (the design called it `ON_BOUND_ITEM_RELEASED`): a bound grant whose `endOnEvents` names it ends when its item is no longer held (`grantsEndedByHolderState`, `itemInstanceIdsOnActor`).
- Room contract: the cast command target carries `boundItemInstanceId`; the `ACTION_ITEM_CHOICES` read lists the items a chosen-item action may be used on. A Character cast that needs an item and names none, or names one it does not take, is refused before it rolls (`TARGET_UNAVAILABLE`, "Choose the item this action is used on.", `encounterChosenItemRefusal`). Commit persistence re-applies the target's release from the room's `targetItemUseStateTransition` and checks it, so the drop is stored.
- Damage: a grant-set weapon die rides the DAMAGE mechanic as `weaponDieFaces` and the `dnd5e.weapon-damage-die` fact, so the authored-dice check accepts the weapon's own dice with that die (Shillelagh's d8).
- Other data: the Abilities field of `OPTIONAL_ABILITY_MODIFIER_BONUS` offers Spellcasting; METAL is in the weapon and armor property lists; Vicious Mockery's grant is gated to its failed save (`ON_SAVE_FAIL`).
- Shillelagh: a minute on a club or quarterstaff, d8 damage die, spellcasting ability for attack and damage, magical, ending when it is let go. Magic Weapon: +1/+2/+3 by slot (levels 2/4/6) on a nonmagical weapon, magical; no release ends it. Heat Metal: Constitution save on a METAL item (METAL tags added to metal weapons and armor), 2d8 fire per slot either way; on a failure a held item is dropped, and disadvantage on attacks and ability checks applies until the source's next turn unless it was dropped. `SHILLELAGH`, `MAGIC_WEAPON` and `HEAT_METAL` leave `CLOSED_RULE_KIND`.
- Moved out: EQUIPMENT_MAGIC_AMMUNITION to ATTACK-19.T4; Heat Metal's later-turn bonus-action repeat to T2l (MANUAL_ADJUDICATION).
- Proofs: `coreOptionSpellAuthoringParity.test.ts` (three spells), `characterSpellCastActions.test.ts` (plans, Magic Weapon slot scaling, Shillelagh from core options only, refusals), `grantLifetimes.test.ts` (bound grant ends when its item is not held, stays when held or with no release event), `encounterDisarmSettlement.test.ts` (releasable means held; filters; revert), `coreOptionSpells.test.ts`, `apps/session-worker/test/nativeRoomCoreOptionSpells.test.ts` (Shillelagh's Club attack deals d8 + STR; Heat Metal on a held longsword drops it with no disadvantage and undo restores it, on worn Chain Mail it stays worn with disadvantage, on a success it stays held with disadvantage).

### T2k3 (Sanctuary, D156 T2 class B)

- Sanctuary is an `APPLY_GRANT` of the `INCOMING_ATTACK_GATE` `ENABLE` modifier (`{savingThrow}`, `SOURCE_SAVE_DC`, Wisdom) for 1 minute, ending when its holder makes an attack roll or casts a spell that affects an enemy (`ATTACK_ROLL` and `SPELL_CAST` end triggers against `ENEMY`; the guard in `effectActionCompiler.ts` widened to allow both). `SANCTUARY` leaves `CLOSED_RULE_KIND`.
- Ruling: the gate covers an attack or a harmful spell aimed at the warded creature, never an area that catches it. A failed save loses the action, but the action, slot, resource and item stay spent and the receipt (`ENCOUNTER_GATED_ACTION`) says so. A failed save first offers the attacker a new target (T2k3b).
- Runtime: `heldIncomingGate` and `incomingGateOffer` (`encounterIncomingAttackGate.ts`) freeze the attacker's Wisdom save against the caster's current spell save DC. `advanceNativeIncomingGate` runs in the single-target path after the currentness check and before the cast-reaction pause: a prompted player gets a d20 pause (`incomingGate` on the pending context, `d20Policy` null), an auto-roll player or a creature is rolled server-side. A pass is carried as `passedIncomingGate` on every later pending snapshot, so replays skip the gate; the pause continuity rule lives in `castPendingContinues`. A fail is terminal: `planNativeGatedAction` (spend through the shared `planNativeLostSourceAction`, also used by the countered cast) commits through `persistEncounterGatedActionCommit`. Undo restores through the existing lost-root path in `actionRevert.ts`.
- Proofs: `encounterIncomingAttackGate.test.ts` (ward lookup, lowest id), `nativeRoomSanctuary.test.ts` (room journey: pass, fail with the spent action, prompted pass and fail, undo, area not gated, ward ends when the holder attacks).
- Scope gaps (in [future-enhancements](../roadmap/future-enhancements.md#deferred-edge-cases-watch-for-in-real-use)): a server-rolled save leaves no roll card; chosen-list `ACTORS` spells are not gated; the DC is read when the attack is made, not pinned at cast; a creature caster of Sanctuary needs a spell save DC on its stat block; the pause prompt carries no trigger label; only `SOURCE_SAVE_DC` gates are supported.

### T2k3b (Sanctuary new target, D156 T2 class B)

- Ruling: a failed ward save does not lose the action at once. The attacker (a prompted player on their own roll, or anyone for a server-rolled save) is offered every other creature, except the attacker and the holder, as a new target, or may decline and lose the action. Nothing is Sanctuary-specific: the pause belongs to the generic `INCOMING_ATTACK_GATE`.
- Runtime: a failed `advanceNativeIncomingGate` on a single-target (`ACTOR`) command with candidates returns the `RETARGET_PENDING` plan (`encounterAttackRetarget.ts` in rules-runtime: `encounterAttackRetargetOffer`, the System-owned bounded choice, `retargetEncounterAction`). The sealed snapshot carries `attackRetarget` `{gate, options}` on the `ENCOUNTER_ACTION` context (mutually exclusive with every other pause) and the summary is `ATTACK_RETARGET_CHOICE`; each option id is a checksum of the ward and the creature (`encounterAttackRetargetUseOptionId`), validated by the offer schema. The answer is `RESOLVE_RULE_OCCURRENCE` with `DECLINE` or `OPTION{optionId}`.
- Answer: a decline replays the stored command with the sealed failed gate (no second roll) into the same `ENCOUNTER_GATED_ACTION` commit, whose fence checks the offer's d20 against the root. An option swaps `target.actorId` and `sceneContext.targetTokenId` (`liveActionAdmission` and the worker derive the same retargeted command and fingerprint), so the action goes through the ordinary pipeline against the new creature: range and line of sight warnings, visibility (`requireTargetSeenByMember`), cast reactions, and the new creature's own ward are all evaluated afresh. The currentness checksum is exempt on this one transition because the target is meant to change; `castPendingContinues` allows only gate → retarget, retarget → chosen creature's pause, and nothing carried across.
- Proofs: `encounterAttackRetarget.test.ts` (option derivation, offer contract, choice, retargeted command), `actionPendingOffers.test.ts` (continuity), `nativeRoomSanctuary.test.ts` (room journeys: offer lists the other creatures, retarget lands on the new creature, retarget passes or fails the second ward and offers again, decline loses the action), `CampaignTabletopDecisionDrawer.test.tsx`.
- Scope gaps are in [future-enhancements](../roadmap/future-enhancements.md#deferred-edge-cases-watch-for-in-real-use).

### T2k3c (retarget offer redaction, D156 T2 class B)

### T2d (rolled damage dice, D156)

- Contract: `ADD_BONUS_DIE` on `DAMAGE` (`{count, faces, sign, recipient, damageTypeId | damageTypeSource: 'ATTACK', countPerCastLevelAbove?}`). `recipient` is `HOLDER_DEALS` or `SOURCE_DEALS_TO_HOLDER` (a mark: only the effect's source gains the die against its holder). An effect's `lifecycle.consumeOnFirstEvents: ['ON_HIT']` ends it on the holder's first hit. `againstEffectSource` on an `ATTACK_ROLL` modifier limits it to attacks against the effect's source. Divine Favor, Branding Smite, Hunter's Mark, Enlarge/Reduce (a cast choice) and Bestow Curse (a cast choice on a failed save) author it from those options; their five closed kinds are deleted, and `grantAuthoringParity` proves each spell.
- Evaluation: the damage compiler adds one damage node per die (`added-<k>-`), rolled with the hit's dice and doubled on a critical; a subtracted die splices in a damage reduction, floored at 0 (the System's floor), and a zero-damage hit applies nothing. Transport: `damageAddedDice` on the mechanics batch output; the roll card labels each die's damage with its source (`docs/architecture/transport.md`).
- Proofs: `encounterDamageDice.test.ts` (each spell, critical, miss, mark by another, cast level, Reduce floor, Bestow's disadvantage only against its caster), `encounterActiveGrants.test.ts` (the curse leaves the Character's attack modes normal), `nativeRoomDamageDice.test.ts` (room journey: Hunter's Mark carded under the spell; Branding Smite deals its dice and ends on the hit).
- Deferred edge cases (in [future-enhancements](../roadmap/future-enhancements.md#deferred-edge-cases-watch-for-in-real-use)): Enlarge/Reduce's size change is description only; Branding Smite's light and visibility are description only; Hunter's Mark's Perception/Survival advantage is description only (its re-mark is the `effectOffers` move, SPELLS-4.T2d2); ON_HIT consumption is wired on the single-target attack path only, and the early-return outcome branches add no dice; Reduce's damage floors at 0 (the SRD says 1, ruled to the System floor); Bestow Curse's lost-action option is a GM ruling, because no option gives a held effect a start-of-turn save that repeats every turn and loses that turn's action on a failure.

### T2c2 (ability-check dice and Guidance, D156; D156 ruling "T2c2a")

- Scope (orchestrator ruling): ability checks, Guidance and the Sheet. Saves and Resistance moved to **SPELLS-4.T2c2b** (consumption narrowed by ability through one shared seam across every save commit site); `RESISTANCE` stays in `CLOSED_RULE_KIND` until then (removed in T2c2b).
- Contract: `ABILITY_CHECK` accepts `ADD_BONUS_DIE` (`{count, faces, sign}`, optional `ability`). Guidance is an `APPLY_GRANT` of one d4 on ability checks with `consumeOnFirstEvents: ['ON_ABILITY_CHECK']`; `GUIDANCE` leaves `CLOSED_RULE_KIND`. `grantAuthoringParity` proves it.
- Consumption narrowing: `effectsEndedBy` takes a `ScopedAbilityCheck` (`{event: 'ON_ABILITY_CHECK', abilityCode}`) and spends only the effects whose ability-check grant applied to that check; a bare `ON_ABILITY_CHECK` never spends a die-bearing effect (a contest rolls no dice yet). Effects with no ability-check grant (Help) are spent whenever they list the event. Regression: `grantLifetimes.test.ts` "effectsEndedBy for an ability check".
- Encounter: First Aid, Hide/Search and Escape checks (basic-action, concealment, escape) add the die through the D20 added-dice graph (`dnd5eAddedDiceNode` in the basic-check definition) and spend only the dice that applied. The die auto-spends on the first qualifying check.
- Sheet: an ability or skill roll adds the Character's carried die (`carriedCheckDice`, `createRollRecord` `addedDice`) after advantage keeps one d20, and spends the effect in the same mutation. Proof: `rollsCarriedCheckDice.test.ts` (a carried Guidance effect). Sheet-cast effect creation is Q27 and unchanged.
- Deferred edge cases (rare, recoverable):
  - The roller cannot choose to save the die for a later check; it is spent on the first qualifying check.
  - Contests (passage/contest checks) compute the die (`ContestFacts.addedDice`) but their graphs do not roll it yet; they do not spend a held die.
  - The attack-roll `ON_ATTACK_ROLL` spend is not narrowed by attack domain or ability.
  - The Encounter cast-reaction check carries no dice.
  - A Sheet-consumed die whose Concentration belongs to another caster leaves that caster's Concentration standing.

#### T2i2 (damage interception contract; T2i2a and T2i2b built)

Generic "damage that would drop the holder to 0 hit points leaves it at N instead, with uses". Consumers: Death Ward (T2i2b), Undead Fortitude (SPELLS-33.T4i), Relentless Endurance.

- **Authoring**: a modifier grant `valueDomainCode: 'DAMAGE_INTERCEPTION'`, `modifierKind: 'ENABLE'`, payload `{ dropToHitPoints: 1..1000, negatesKill: boolean }` (`damageInterceptionPayloadDtoSchema`; node map in `grants/maps/modifier.ts`). The holding effect's `lifecycle.uses` is the number of interceptions; the last one ends the effect. Polarity BENEFICIAL.
- **Uses extension point (documented only, not built)**: a later `uses: { kind: 'RESOURCE', resourceId }` would spend a class/feature resource instead of `remainingUses` (Relentless Endurance: once per long rest; Undead Fortitude: per trigger). Add it beside the payload when the first consumer needs it; `heldDamageInterceptions` is the one place that decides what is "unspent".
- **Runtime** (`rules-runtime/src/encounterDamageInterception.ts`, pure, shared by every actor kind):
  - `heldDamageInterceptions(effects)`: unspent interceptions, lowest effect id first (replay-stable); an effect with `remainingUses < 1` holds none.
  - `hitPointsAfterDamage`: damage taking the holder from above 0 to exactly 0 leaves `min(dropToHitPoints, maximum)`; anything else is plain subtraction.
  - `applyDamageInterceptions`: the first lethal damage application or `KILL` spends each interception in order; later damage is rebased on the hit points the interception left, so a second blow meets the ward spent. A `KILL` with an unspent `negatesKill` interception becomes `KILL_NEGATED`.
- **Where it is applied** (T2i2b: one role each): `planEncounterMechanicsBatch` applies the interceptions (`applyDamageInterceptions`, all three branches: Run creature, Character, Character in a form) from the pools the target starts from, and `foldCharacterMechanicsApplications` only validates the result (optional input `damageInterceptions`): each intercepted blow must match what the first unspent interception makes of it, a lethal blow or `KILL` a ward could have settled is refused, and a forged `interceptedBy` is refused. Healing and temporary hit points after an interception are rebased generically onto the running hit point and temporary pools (`temporaryHitPointsAfterGrant` / `AfterLoss` / `AfterRelease`), so any order of changes in one batch settles.
- **Receipts** (`ruleExecutionMechanics.ts`): `DAMAGE.interceptedBy` (the spent effect id; the validators accept `1 <= after <= maximum` when set, and an intercepted blow does not touch death saves, nor trigger the massive-damage instant death) and `KILL_NEGATED { negatedBy }` (no state change). Both are validated in the fold and in the receipt chains.
- **Proofs**: `encounterDamageInterception.test.ts` runs every case for a Character with death saves and a Run creature without, using an authored fixture effect.
- **T2i2b (built)**:
  - `heldBatchInputs({ baseMaximumHitPoints, effects })` returns every batch input the held effects derive (`effectiveMaximumHitPoints`, `damageInterceptions`). `MovementTarget` facts carry `damageInterceptions` (required), and every planner caller (action plan, turn effects, spatial movement, native contest, cleaving, check and basic-action responses) passes the facts' value beside `effectiveMaximumHitPoints`.
  - The room applies a committed action's receipt applications through `applyRoomEncounterMechanicsBatch`, which derives the target's `damageInterceptions` from its held effects (`turnEffectOwner`) for every caller. The persistence builders assemble those batches without held effects, so this is the one place the interceptions reach the commit.
  - Persist-side spend/end in `persistEncounterMechanicsBatchPlan` (`spendDamageInterceptions`): each effect named by a receipt's `DAMAGE.interceptedBy` or `KILL_NEGATED.negatedBy` spends one use (`spendEffectUses`) or, with its last use, ends (`deleteGrants`). The original records go into `endedGrants`, so the action's receipt `consumedGrants` and `restoreConsumedGrants` give the use or the ward back on undo (precedent: Mirror Image decoys).
  - Death Ward is data: `lifecycle: { uses: 1 }` and `{ dropToHitPoints: 1, negatesKill: true }`; the `DEATH_WARD` closed-rule kind is deleted and Death Ward is in the `coreOptionSpells.test.ts` parity table.
  - Proofs: `encounterDamageInterception.test.ts` (fold-only regression: a lethal blow no interception settled now throws; the generic rebase; a forged `interceptedBy`; `heldBatchInputs`), `encounterMechanicsBatchPlan.test.ts` (the planner applies the ward for a Run creature and a Character), `coreOptionSpells.test.ts`, and the room journey `apps/session-worker/test/nativeRoomDeathWard.test.ts` (warded Character takes lethal damage, stays at 1 hit point, the ward ends; a two-use ward keeps one; undo restores hit points and ward).
