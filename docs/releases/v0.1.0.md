## What shipped

Ledger status (`docs/roadmap/08-phase-ledger.json`): 21 features, every PBI DONE except PERF-2 (TODO), RELEASE-3 (staging promotion, TODO), RELEASE-4 (production, TODO) and RELEASE-7 (alpha play guide, IN_PROGRESS).

- **Platform (28 PBIs).** Convex is the only product-data store; Supabase, SQL/Kysely, tRPC and GoRules are retired. Web app on Cloudflare Workers (ADR 0037), Session Worker and Durable Object for live tables (ADR 0035), R2 media (ADR 0012), Resend email. Owner delete of every owned record (PLATFORM-26, ADR 0040). Authorization sweep (PLATFORM-27). CI that selects the tests a change can reach, red-proofs new tests, and auto-fixes format and generated files ([CI validation](../architecture/ci-validation.md)).
- **Accounts (12).** Sign-up closed by default, verified email, invitations (including invitation sign-in), login, password reset, usernames, profile privacy, superadmin, account deletion, password and email change.
- **Authoring (21).** Game Systems; authoring and publish for Background, Class, Creature, Equipment, Feat, Race, Spell, Vehicle and effects; drafts, duplication, sharing. Master and seeded records are locked and copyable into owned drafts.
- **Rules (2).** The node-runtime kernel and the composable, System-neutral engine (ADR 0023, ADR 0039).
- **Builder and Sheet.** Builder owns structural Character changes; Sheet owns play-state (8 Sheet PBIs).
- **Campaign, Social, Room, Scene.** Campaigns (8), social discovery and listings, live Rooms (7), Scenes with tokens, walls, doors, terrain, fog and light.
- **Encounters, Move, Attack, Spells, Reaction.** Encounters (10), movement and reactions including System-authored opportunity attacks (D231), attacks (18), the SRD spell corpus (35 PBIs), reaction offers and choices.
- **Privacy, Reliability, VTT UI, Quality.** Privacy rulings (D211), reliability (24), the VTT surface, and the quality audit slices (18).

Counts are PBIs per feature in the ledger.

## Architecture and decisions

Load-bearing records: ADRs [0023](../adr/0023-shared-rules-kernel-and-system-capabilities.md), [0024](../adr/0024-current-published-authored-content.md), [0033](../adr/0033-campaign-owned-play-and-session-log.md), [0035](../adr/0035-room-authoritative-live-encounters.md), [0036](../adr/0036-versioned-content-snapshots-and-browser-builder.md), [0037](../adr/0037-web-app-on-cloudflare-workers.md), [0039](../adr/0039-composable-system-neutral-rules-engine.md). Decisions by number are in the [log](v0.1.0-decisions.md); the ones a reader most often needs:

- D63 defeat at 0 HP, D75 skeleton SSR for signed-in pages, D156 description-only rule rubric.
- D209 Q16 I/O budget (4.3 MB per session), D210 latency recorded locally and gated on staging, D229 and D230 the engineering that met the I/O budget.
- D211 authorization rulings, D212 and D235 UAT shape, D213 the pre-staging gate, D216 Convex 1 s timeouts are concurrency bursts (about 20 executions start within 25 ms).
- D231 opportunity attacks as a System-authored reaction, D232 default remote JWKS, D233 Workers Paid at production cutover and outcome-based C2, D234 PERF-2 closes on CI budgets plus staging evidence.

Seed and replace margins (Convex mutation 1 s limit):

- **Seed (PLATFORM-5.T1).** `importSubjects` was 377 ms idle and 1005 ms timed out under load; after cost-budget batching it is 151 ms idle and 308 ms loaded. The budget is 25 per record plus 1 per KiB, 100 per batch, and a Class sits alone in its batch.
- **Replace (PLATFORM-18.T1).** Replacing a small record costs about 100 to 150 ms against about 28 ms for an insert. Replaced subjects get a batch alone, and `readSubjectChecksums` and `changedSubjectIds` pre-read the stored checksums so unchanged records skip the write. The largest Class replace measured 242 ms.

Authorization sweep (PLATFORM-27.T1): 295 public Convex functions (136 queries, 140 mutations, 19 actions) and 14 HTTP routes, classified by access rule: self 61, owner 65, member 77, gm 67, superadmin 5, signedIn 8, public 9, development 5. `apps/apis/convex/publicFunctionAccess.test.ts` fails when a function is added without a rule. The seven rulings are D211.

Other recorded budgets: PERF-1.T5 Sheet command budget; PERF-4.T1 stat-block cost at the actor cap (a 99-creature pass is about 22 ms p50); Q16 I/O met by engineering (D229, D230).
