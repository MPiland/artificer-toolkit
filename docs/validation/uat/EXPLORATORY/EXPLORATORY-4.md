# EXPLORATORY-4: Run a short session as a first-time player

Join a Campaign you have never seen, find your Character and take your first turn.

Level 0 (Orientation and exploratory tasks) · Feature EXPLORATORY · Run order 5 of 188

Do not open the scripted scenario files before your Result is written: they name every button and would hide the confusion this task exists to find. This task is not pass/fail.

## Prerequisites

- The review data provides the account SPARE2, who is in no Campaign, and the Campaign **Review: Shield and Hold** (Encounter running).

## Accounts and windows

- **Accounts:** SPARE2 (`Review_Pl_Eli`), with the GM in a second browser profile
- **Windows:** One browser profile per account ([how](../README.md#running-several-users-side-by-side)).

## What is ready for you

- Before you start (operator): **GM** invites `Review_Pl_Eli` to Review: Shield and Hold by username from their profile. **SPARE2 accepts the invitation themselves** as part of the task; the operator does not do it for them.

## Task

- **Goal:** Join a Campaign you have never seen, find your Character and take your first turn.
- **Scope:** Accept the invitation, open the Campaign, and take a turn in the running Encounter. You may use the [alpha play guide](../../../guides/alpha-play-guide.md). If you need a Character, build one.
- **Look for:** whether the invitation and joining steps were clear; whether you could tell whose turn it was; whether you could find the action you wanted without help.

Follow [how to run a task](README.md#how-to-run-a-task) and write the Result below when you stop.

## Afterwards

- **GM:** Remove SPARE2 from Review: Shield and Hold (Party panel) so the account is again in no Campaign.
