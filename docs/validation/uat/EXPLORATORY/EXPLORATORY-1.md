# EXPLORATORY-1: Build a level-3 caster and play one round

Create a new level-3 spellcasting Character and play one round of combat with it.

Level 3 (Core play) · Feature EXPLORATORY · Mode: Group (P3 is the tester, GM follows the script) · Path position 82 of 191

Do not open the scripted scenario files before your Result is written: they name every button and would hide the confusion this task exists to find. This task is not pass/fail.

## Prerequisites

- The account **P3** exists and is seated in The Campaign. P3 has Cade as their Campaign Character: a Level 1 Fighter that P3 built in the Character Builder.
- **The Campaign** is hosted by GM, in Assisted mode, with a Wolf and a Goblin and an Encounter in round 2.

## Accounts and windows

- **Roles used:** P3, GM
- **Windows:** Each role is a different person in their own browser, signed in to their own account.
- **Tester:** P3, in their own browser. You never act as another role.
- **GM:** a second person, in their own browser and signed in to their own GM account, following the script below. They do only what the script says, when the trigger happens, and do not explain controls.

## Script for the second person

1. Before the tester starts: GM opens The Campaign. Do nothing else.
2. When the tester says their new Character is committed: GM opens the Party panel and waits. The tester assigns the new Character to their own seat with **Change Character**; GM confirms it shows on the roster.
3. When the tester says they are ready to play: GM adds the Character to the running Encounter (a token and an initiative entry) and tells the tester when it is their turn.
4. Keep acting only on the tester's say-so. Do not explain controls; note every question the tester asks in the Result.

## Task

- **Goal:** Create a new level-3 spellcasting Character and play one round of combat with it.
- **Scope:** Choose any caster Class and Race you like. Pick spells without being told which. End when your Character has acted once in the Encounter round and the second person confirms the result is visible at the table.
- **Look for:** whether you found where to start; whether level, spell slots and spells were easy to set; whether you understood what to do on your turn.

Follow [how to run a task](README.md#how-to-run-a-task) and write the Result below when you stop. Tell the second person in words when a trigger in the script happens (for example "my Character is ready").

## Afterwards

- P3: use **Change Character** to put Cade back on your seat. GM: remove the new Character's token from the Encounter, and P3 deletes the new Character.
