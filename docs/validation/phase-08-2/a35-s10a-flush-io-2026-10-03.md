# A35-S10a checkpoint I/O — 2026-10-03

Measured in `apps/apis/convex/liveSessions/checkpoint.test.ts` (convex-test, local) with temporary
instrumentation that wrote the checkpoint response's `io.sections`; the committed tests keep the
equality and bound assertions. Raw data was disposable (`local-validation/s10a/`).

**Fixture:** a fresh Campaign, the first checkpoint after activation, one Creature actor losing 1 HP.

| Section | Bytes read | Documents read | Bytes written | Documents written |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| fence | 898 | 2 | 834 | 1 |
| records | 5,875 | 7 | 1,159 | 2 |
| scopes | 2,129 | 2 | 0 | 0 |
| holds, scenes, history, privateRolls, remainder | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| **total** | **8,902** | **11** | **1,993** | **3** |

- **Before:** ADR 0035 D86 recorded a floor of about 250 KB per checkpoint, from Campaign-wide
  assignment and membership collects, post-write re-reads, a hold reconcile over every actor and an
  in-checkpoint authorization rebuild. The old code was not re-measured in this run.
- **Roster size:** a Campaign with 36 extra members and assignments matches every counter in every
  section exactly. 36 extra actors leave documents read unchanged (bytes bounded at +3,600 B).
- **Over 12 consecutive checkpoints:** checkpoints 2–12 each read 9,672 B in 11 documents. The
  one-time +770 B is the authority row gaining its persisted state and checksum after the first
  checkpoint. No section grows with checkpoint count.
- **With one history entry per checkpoint:** 9.1 KB read in 12 documents and 5 documents written.
  Receipt-ledger collision and orphan checks are index probes that read no documents.
- **Not measured here:** room-side costs and the full V15 workload; REL-5 reads `sectionIoBytes`
  from the meter.
