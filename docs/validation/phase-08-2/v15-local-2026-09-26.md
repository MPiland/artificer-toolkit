## The workload as updated

`verifyEncounterCutoverWorkload` in `apps/apis/tests/developmentEncounterCutover.ts`, run by
`developmentDrafts.ts --encounter-v15-workload` with `V15_BROWSER_PROOF=1` (lifecycle stage
`r08-browser-v15-workload`):

- **Shape:** one GM and five players; two Campaigns (a primary and an independent aggregate), each with
  an eleven-creature Run on its own presented Scene; eight rounds. Every actor turn has two actions
  (`RESOLVE_ACTION` for the action and the bonus action) and then, for actors 1–10, a bound token move
  through the Session room that provokes the next actor's opportunity reaction, its response and dice
  continuations, and the end of the turn. The eleventh actor's turn only ends: eleven actors on two
  dispositions (D17) cannot each provoke a hostile reactor whose reaction resets before the next round
  needs it, so the schedule uses the criterion's "up to one reaction per actor turn". Actors alternate
  Friendly and Hostile dispositions (D17 makes disposition the source of hostility).
- **Player-issued commands (V15.3):** the GM controls actor 1 and each other actor is given to a
  player (`UPDATE_ACTOR controllerUserIds`). Every command comes from its actor's controller through
  that member's own room socket: actions, the move, the end of the turn, and the reaction response
  from the reactor's controller. Every client keeps its rooms open like a tabletop tab and refreshes
  its token each round.
- **V15.1 checks (preflight on the primary aggregate):** a same-version `RESOLVE_ACTION` race between
  two GM clients (one winner, one stale loser, exact winner replay leaving projection and timeline
  unchanged); a refused out-of-turn action and a refused player command for the GM's actor
  (`NOT_ACTOR_CONTROLLER`) that leave no trace; a `SET_INITIATIVE` transaction forced to roll back
  (`encounters/developmentLifecycle:verifyRollback`); a reaction whose pending decision only the
  reacting actor's player can read; the reaction's single mechanics receipt. The two aggregates run
  their schedules concurrently (unrelated concurrency).
- **V15.2 metrics:** gameplay command time per operation from each command's send to its
  acknowledgement; warm durable convergence from the committing command's acknowledgement (Convex has
  committed before the room acknowledges) to the slowest player's Convex subscription delivery of that
  Run version, and in the six browsers to each peer's paint of the Scene version; ephemeral preview
  from the GM's shared drag preview to each peer's paint. The workload logs
  `V15 workload measurement: {...}` before any threshold judges it.

## Blocking findings

1. **Backend queries exceed the 1 s limit under the workload.** With six tabletops open, Encounter
   projection and scene-binding reads and the room's rules capture run past Convex's 1 s query limit.
   A timed-out capture reaches the player as `STALE_STATE` ("The Encounter changed"), which hides the
   cause. The machine was memory-bound during these runs (0.1–0.5 GB free of 15 GB; the Convex
   container at 4.3–5.9 GiB), so the local numbers overstate managed latency, but the same reads were
   already near the limit in the [command timing](command-timing-2026-09-26.md) measurement.
2. **A room stopped answering a reaction response** for 120 s in round 7 of `diag5`.
3. **Workload Campaign deletion** failed ("Campaign cleanup failed") or timed out at `DELETING` after
   the diagnostic runs, leaving fixture Campaigns in the local database until the next reset.
