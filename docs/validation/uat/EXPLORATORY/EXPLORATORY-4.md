# EXPLORATORY-4: Run a short session as a first-time player

Join a Campaign you have never seen, find your Character and take your first turn.

Level 3 (Core play) · Feature EXPLORATORY · Mode: Group (P5 is the tester, GM follows the script) · Path position 86 of 195

Do not open the scripted scenario files before your Result is written: they name every button and would hide the confusion this task exists to find. This task is not pass/fail.

## Prerequisites

- The account **P5** exists, is a member of P3's Campaign only (see [SOCIAL-2](../SOCIAL/UAT-SOCIAL-2.md)) and has built no Character.
- **The Campaign** is hosted by GM with P1 to P4 seated (one of its five player seats is free), with an Encounter running.

## Accounts and windows

- **Roles used:** P5, GM
- **Windows:** Each role is a different person in their own browser, signed in to their own account.
- **Tester:** P5, in their own browser. You never act as another role.
- **GM:** a second person, in their own browser and signed in to their own GM account, following the script below. They do only what the script says, when the trigger happens, and do not explain controls.

## Script for the second person

1. Before the tester starts: GM opens The Campaign and invites P5 by username.
2. When the tester says they accepted the invitation: GM confirms P5 is on the Party roster. P5 picks their own Character with **Add Character** (P5 builds one if asked).
3. When the tester says they are ready to play: GM makes P5's Character the active actor and tells the tester when it is their turn.

## Task

- **Goal:** Join a Campaign you have never seen, find your Character and take your first turn.
- **Scope:** Accept the invitation, open the Campaign, and take a turn in the running Encounter. You may use the [alpha play guide](../../../guides/alpha-play-guide.md). If you need a Character, build one.
- **Look for:** whether the invitation and joining steps were clear; whether you could tell whose turn it was; whether you could find the action you wanted without help.

Follow [how to run a task](README.md#how-to-run-a-task) and write the Result below when you stop. Tell the second person in words when a trigger in the script happens (for example "my Character is ready").

## Afterwards

- GM: remove P5 from The Campaign (Party panel) and delete any Character built for P5, so P5 is again a member of P3's Campaign only.

None. Exploratory tasks check usability and bias, not acceptance criteria.
