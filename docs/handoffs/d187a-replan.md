# D187a re-plan: per-task scope

Status: current (2026-10-23). This is the source for the re-sized SPELLS-4, SPELLS-8, SPELLS-30, SPELLS-31 and SPELLS-33 tasks. The decision is D187a in [phase-08-2-decisions.md](phase-08-2-decisions.md).

## Rules for a session picking up a re-sized task

- Riders marked DOC, DESCRIPTION_ONLY or "cut" below ship as their description, shown as a GM ruling (MANUAL_ADJUDICATION). Follow the existing precedents: `bestow-curse.ts` and `command.ts` in `packages/source-data/src/srd/spells`. Delete the closed-rule kind and its resolver, and keep or add an authoring-parity proof.
- Do not build a deferred engine piece. Each one is listed in [future-enhancements](../roadmap/future-enhancements.md) under "Deferred rules pieces (D187a)".
- Riders marked FIT or NEEDS, and anything in the overrides, are still automated with core options.
- If a task turns out bigger than its ledger size, report to the orchestrator instead of widening it (D187).

## Overrides (user rulings and the consistency check)

- Kept automated at full size: SPELLS-4.T2s (Wild Shape forms), T2r (non-save auras), T2f1b (the Dispel Magic above-level check), T2m1b2c (Concentration on zone damage) and T2v (Spiritual Weapon); SPELLS-33.T6d1 (Sunlight Sensitivity) and T5n2 (Charge); SPELLS-31.T4s (Wild Shape join) and T4k2d (Tranquility, per the 2026-10-19 ruling).
- SPELLS-4.T2m2: keep the Freedom of Movement PREVENT half, using CONDITION_IMMUNITY as in `heroism.ts` and `mind-blank.ts`. Calm Emotions SUPPRESS becomes description.
- SPELLS-4.T2p: Gaseous Form is authored from fly SET_VALUE, DAMAGE_RESISTANCE and MOVEMENT_MODE if it fits within the size. Ray of Enfeeblement becomes description.
- SPELLS-31.T5n2: the One with Shadows light gate reuses the illumination predicate that SPELLS-33.T6d1 builds. Otherwise it is description.
- SPELLS-33.T4i4b: save-gated drop to 0 HP. Reuse the save-gated KILL (SPELLS-33.T4i3b) and the REDUCED_TO_ZERO_BY gate; add only the REDUCE_TO_ZERO_HIT_POINTS outcome.
- SPELLS-33.T6a3: Stirge and Mimic attach reuse the Darkmantle ACTOR_RELATIONSHIP pattern (`darkmantle.ts`, `rug-of-smothering.ts`). Marilith Tail and Roper Reel become description.
- SPELLS-33.T6a1: includes the one proof that a creature ON_HIT attack with damage plus MANUAL_ADJUDICATION compiles through the attack sweep, and removes those allowlist entries. Swallow, engulf (T6a2), curse (T6b1), lycanthropy (T6b2), disease (T6b3) and healing-block wounds (T6m2) are GM rulings beside the damage.
- SPELLS-4.T2g2 (Raise Dead) and T2lb2b1 (Eyebite, Break Enchantment) are description; see their task text.
