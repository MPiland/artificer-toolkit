## Scope after the split

A Character casts its spells in an Encounter through `RESOLVE_ACTION`, like a Feature action. T1 now delivers:

1. **Spell action choices.** One choice per `spellCastCandidates` entry (one per offered cast level), id `spell-cast:<uuidv5(candidateId)>` (fixed length), labelled with the spell and level; a cantrip once.
2. **Slot spend and GM undo.** A leveled cast spends the slot (or pact slot) in the same Character version as its outcome; GM undo restores it. Single-target roots, and point-area roots (D103: the area root holds the spend once; the caster-caught child batch carries it).
3. **Upcasting.** Any slot at or above the spell's level is offered, as the Sheet offers it (`getExplicitSpellCastLevels`); the cast level is bound as the fact `dnd5e.spell-cast-level` on the compiled action and the dnd5e compiler scales by that fact. A pact slot casts a lower-level spell.

T1 delivers **part of** AC SPELLS-8.1. It does not close it.

Registered as separate tasks (not built here): Concentration start for every SRD Concentration spell, multi-Effect casts, components, LIMITED_USE and pool-backed casts, mixed or non-standard casting times, scaling heals with creature-type exclusions, rituals.

Read first: D95, D99, D103, D104 in `docs/handoffs/phase-08-2-decisions.md`; `docs/architecture/rules-runtime.md`; ADR 0034.

## Design (as built)

- `buildCharacterSpellCastActions` (`packages/rules-runtime/src/character/characterSpellCastActions.ts`) turns the Sheet's declarations into Encounter actions. It reuses the Sheet's own logic: `isActiveSpellSource` (per class, ALWAYS_PREPARED and GRANTED sources), `getExplicitSpellCastLevels`, `getOwnedSpellSlotLevels`, `readSpellCastSlot`. Each spell's Effects are compiled once per load (WeakMap cache).
- Spell data rides on the action/cast source, not on the movement-reaction source.
- The slot is spent and restored with the Sheet's `updateSpellSlotUsage` (spend in `characterNativeActionResources.ts`, restore in `actionRevert.ts`). The restore is fenced by the Character `streamVersion`; a Sheet change after the cast refuses undo.
- The dnd5e compiler reads the cast level from the declared fact `DND5E_SPELL_CAST_LEVEL_FACT_KEY` (`dnd5e.spell-cast-level`), declared in the Game System `factContracts`. The old reverse match on `exactDiceConfiguration` is gone.
- `actionChoiceReads.ts` maps candidates and spell casts through one path; the damage label includes the damage type.
- Test helper `packages/rules-runtime/src/testing/fixedHealSpell.ts`: a non-damage spell the compiler can execute (Cure Wounds with an exact heal and unlimited range). Until T6a, real Cure Wounds upcast compiled `MANUAL` and was not offered; T6a offers it (see the T6a section).

## T5: every SRD Concentration spell starts Concentration (Hold Person replaces Fly)

Phase 8 ruling "do both": (a) derive the Concentration maintenance from the spell, (b) make Hold Person compile, with no parallel plan.

- **(a) Derived Concentration.** `compileSpell` marks each compiled Effect group `requiresConcentration` when the spell has the CONCENTRATION attribute (`CompiledEffectGroup.requiresConcentration`). `maintenancePlan(action, entry)` in `effectActionCompiler.ts` builds the Concentration maintenance from that mark; an authored `maintenanceDefinitionId` stays as an override. The explicit id is dropped from Fly, Shield of Faith, Heroism and Protection from Energy. A Concentration spell with no supported maintained effect stays MANUAL ("D&D Concentration spell has no supported maintained effect.").
- **(b) Hold Person.** Authored through the existing follow-up-save shape: a SEQUENCE result block (id is a UUID, required by `repeatingDirectFollowUpSavePlan`) with CONDITION PARALYZED, then FOLLOW_UP_SAVE (`TARGET_TURN_END`, SOURCE_SAVE_DC WISDOM, ONCE with repeat on failure, success ends, failure continues).
- **One DC path.** `pinnedSaveDc(savingThrow)` serves the timed-condition plan, the immediate repeat-save plan and the direct repeat-save plan. FIXED_DC is pinned in `adapterFacts`; SOURCE_SAVE_DC sets the new plan field `castSaveDcFactKey` (`dnd5e.save-dc`), stamped at cast time from the cast roll's DC in `prepareRecurringActiveEffect` (it refuses without the roll). The repeat-save graph reads the threshold from that fact.
- **Changed gate (flagged for review):** `encounterActionEntryPointCompiler.ts` TEST/OUTCOME actions used to combine only with grant plans; they now also admit `conditionContribution` (repeated-save) plans. Message: "TEST/OUTCOME actions combine only with grant or repeated-save active-effect plans."
- **Proof.** rules-runtime `characterSpellCastActions.test.ts` (mark, derived parity for an author-built Concentration spell with no explicit id, Hold Person compiles with maintenance and a condition plan, a non-Concentration spell gets no maintenance) and `grantLifetimes.test.ts` (plans compiled through `compileSpell`). Convex `actionSpellCasting.test.ts` `Hold Person`: a Character casts Fly, then Hold Person; Fly's grant is gone, the Wolf is Paralyzed after a failed save, a failed repeat save holds it, a natural 20 repeat save ends the condition and Concentration; recasting Fly drops the Hold Person condition. Light lane: rules-runtime 78/78, Convex `actionSpellCasting.test.ts` 16/16.
- **Not in T5:** multi-Effect casts (T7), LIMITED_USE or pool casts (T8), mixed casting times (T9), components, rituals.

## T6a: scaling heals and excluded creature types (SPELLS-8.1, partly)

T6 as ledgered also names Mass Cure Wounds, Mass Healing Word and Prayer of Healing. Those target several chosen creatures; `RESOLVE_ACTION`'s target union has no list-of-actors kind (CHOSEN_CREATURES is unsupported), so they cannot be cast in an Encounter without a transport contract change and per-target execution. T6a delivers the single-target half; the multi-target half is proposed as T6b and needs an architect ruling (see the report to Phase 8).

- **Cast-level heal.** `effectAtCastLevel` (`characterSpellCastActions.ts`) turns a `PER_LEVEL` `SPELL_CAST_LEVEL` dice heal into the unscaled row at or below the candidate's cast level before its entry point is built. The compiler's `supported()` keeps its unscaled-heal restriction; a heal's dice count is structural (one `evidence.integer-range` node per die), so it cannot be specialized after the fact the way a damage upcast is (`TEST_OUTCOME`, unchanged).
- **Excluded creature types.** The dnd5e compiler no longer refuses a single-target policy (ACTION, and the single-target ATTACK/SAVE policy) because the Effect excludes creature types. The room already raises the overridable `TARGET_CREATURE_TYPE_EXCLUDED` warning for a Creature target (`evaluateEncounterActionRuleWarnings`); a Character target's type is not in its facts, so no warning (unchanged). `isSupportedTestArea` still refuses excluded types on an area; no SRD area spell uses them.
- **Changed test expectation (deliberate):** `offers a cast once per spell it can execute...` used to assert a scaling heal is never offered; it now asserts the SRD Cure Wounds is offered at 1st and 2nd level. The former fixture ("Scaling Mend") rolled d2 dice, which the roll parser does not support; it was never executable and its default rewrite is deleted.
- **Proof.** rules-runtime `characterSpellCastActions.test.ts` (`the scaling heal a cast carries`: Cure Wounds at 1, 3, 9 and Healing Word at 1, 4 roll exactly the authored dice). Convex `actionSpellCasting.test.ts` `a scaling heal (SPELLS-8.1)`: the SRD Cure Wounds as authored heals 1d8 plus the modifier at 1st level and 2d8 plus the modifier at 2nd level (distinct queued faces), spending STANDARD:1 then STANDARD:2; the SRD Healing Word heals a Beast Wolf; an Undead Wolf raises `TARGET_CREATURE_TYPE_EXCLUDED`, nothing is healed or spent until it is acknowledged, then it heals.
- **Not claimed:** SPELLS-8.2 (Spare the Dying castable, exclusions applied to targets) is a separate criterion; this change removes one blocker for it but the criterion is not closed here.

## T2: a Character wizard casts SRD Fireball at a point (SPELLS-8.4)

No production change was needed: T1's Character spell cast (slot spend, `CHARACTER_MECHANICS_BATCH` for the caster) and SPELLS-7's point area already carry the real SRD Fireball. The ledger note that no Character spell reaches `RESOLVE_ACTION` predates T1. T2 adds the proof the AC names, in `apps/session-worker/test/nativeRoomSpellCasting.test.ts` (`room Fireball cast by a Character wizard (SPELLS-8.4)`), so it passed on its first run against unchanged production code; there is no red run. The checks discriminate by construction: the queued dice sum to 26 (8d6) and 30 (9d6) while a roll per creature would draw the 6s after the queue; the recorded save DC is 11, which a flat DC, a missing proficiency bonus or Strength, Dexterity, Wisdom or Charisma would not give. Constitution 13 also gives 11, so the DC proof does not tell Intelligence from Constitution; the ability lookup is covered by the rules-runtime save-DC unit tests (see the review note in the status log).

- **Covers:** 3rd-level cast rolls 8d6 and 4th-level 9d6, once for the whole area (every caught creature requests the same amount); the saves wait for the caster and the two Wolves in the 20 ft sphere and not for the Wolf out of reach; every save is against DC 11 (Intelligence 12, +2 proficiency); the spent slot is STANDARD:3 or STANDARD:4; one GM undo restores the slot, every creature's vitals and the caster's hit points (the Wolves' vitals and the caster's hit points are asserted to change after the cast, so the restore is not vacuous).
- **Deviation:** the fixture Character is the session-worker Fighter whose Class carries the Wizard's spellcasting rules (Intelligence), as in T1, not a Wizard Class; its slot table also lists a 3rd and a 4th level slot (added in T6a's `f8818570e`, not in the T2 commit). The ledger note's "Wizard fixture is needed" is met by the Wizard spellcasting rules, not a Wizard Class.
- **Not covered (own tasks):** Fireball through the Convex command path and the web (`RESOLVE_ACTION` over Convex is T1's journey for single targets).
