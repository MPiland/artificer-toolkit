### Scene commands through the room

`DevelopmentHttpClient.executeSceneCommand(command, presentation, replayOfSequence?)` sends token
moves, door changes and move undo through the Campaign's Session room as the tabletop does:
`SYNCHRONIZE_SCENE` at the command's expected Scene version, then `COMMAND` with the room sequence. It
resolves with the room's Scene result (null when the member only gets a version bump) and the session
sequence it carried, which an exact retry repeats. `holdRooms` keeps rooms open like a browser tab.
Driver presentation writes use `scenes/presentations:setLive`, which goes through a live room. The
movement browser proof reads moves, undo and their results from the room socket, and the Effects and
Disarm browser proofs read Encounter commands from it (they read retired Convex mutation frames). The
V15, V14.4 and movement fixtures bind the Encounter to the presented Scene before `START` opens the
room (see finding 1).

### V14.4 real-backend reaction race

`verifyEncounterReactionContention` (`--encounter-reaction-contention`, lifecycle stage
`r08-native-encounter-reaction-contention`): the GM moves the first actor past the next one, whose
opportunity reaction the first player controls, and the GM's second client and that player answer every
response step through the room at once. It requires one winner and one refused loser per step, one
reaction spend, one mechanics receipt, the winner's retry to replay or be refused, the loser's retry to
be refused, the root move's exact retry to return the room's stored receipt, and no state change from
any retry. **Passed** (`r08prep-n9-encounter-reaction-contention`): one response step (auto-roll
settles the dice), GM won, player refused with `VERSION_STALE`; winner retry replayed.

## Findings

1. **Encounter binding is refused while a room is live (product defect, escalated).**
   `encounters/sceneBindings:bind` refuses a binding to the presented Scene while room authority is
   live (`assertConvexOwnsSceneLiveState`, asserted by `campaigns/campaignPlay.test.ts`), and no room
   route binds one. The tabletop binds through that mutation (`CampaignVttStage.tsx`) while its room is
   always live, and a room keeps authority five minutes after its last socket. `encounter-lifecycle`
   fails with "This Scene is currently owned by its Campaign live table." The Effects, Disarm and
   Campaign lifecycle drivers also bind after `START` and are expected to fail the same way (not run).
2. **The frozen V15 schedule was infeasible (fixed within the criterion).** See the V15 record.
3. **Backend queries pass the 1 s limit under load; a capture timeout reads as `STALE_STATE`.** See
   the V15 record.
4. **Room Scene acknowledgements drop the initiator's occurrence** when the initiator cannot see the
   change: `#broadcastSceneCommitted` sends `SCENE_VERSION` before it considers the initiator's
   occurrence. The initiator then learns of its movement decision only through subscriptions.
   Separately, the room's exact replay returns the stored receipt with `replayed: false`, where the
   Convex paths return `replayed: true`.
5. **Two Storybook stories fail deterministically** (reproduced in isolation):
   `CampaignTabletopActionDrawer` › Unavailable Action (dimmed text contrast 3.53:1 against 4.5:1) and
   `CampaignTabletopWindow` › Gm Window (no `Ember` option in the token binding select).
6. **Browser proofs drift from the current auth flow** (table above); not diagnosed.
7. **Local resources.** The Convex container held 4.3–5.9 GiB and the host had 0.1–0.5 GB free during
   the browser runs; a container restart did not reduce it below 4.3 GiB.
